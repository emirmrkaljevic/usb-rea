# Case studies

These are walk-throughs of what usb-rea recovered on a specific device, including the limits and
(where a fixture exists) a command you can run yourself. Two of them use a capture that also
runs in CI.

## [Corsair Hydro H100i Platinum (1b1c:0c18)](corsair-hydro-platinum.md)

Packets were reconstructed from liquidctl's open-source Hydro Platinum driver and written to a
pcap. usb-rea, given no prior knowledge of the protocol, recovered the `0x3f` prefix, the rolling
sequence counter, the fan duty scale, the pump mode byte and the CRC8, and the generated command
matched liquidctl on values that were never in the capture.

## [ASUS TUF Gaming LC III LCD (0b05:1c7b)](asus-tuf-lc3.md)

A real Windows USBPcap capture, shared by the owner in liquidctl#936 and kept as a trimmed
fixture. usb-rea found the brightness command on interrupt EP 0x01 (byte 4 is the percent) and
the 320x320 JPEG stream on EP 0x03, including the per-frame ack, matching what the owner had
worked out by hand.

## [Two bugs a real USBPcap capture found](real-capture-two-bugs.md)

The same LC III fixture is also the first capture that was not generated by usb-rea itself.
Turning it into a regression test exposed two parser bugs in v0.7.5: USBPcap control replies
that arrive in the "complete" record were dropped, so the VID:PID was missing, and acks were
counted over the whole capture, so leftover acks hid the per-frame ack and were mistaken for a
reply to brightness. Both are fixed in v0.7.6.

## [Jungle Leopard HD 9.16" panel (33c3:7788)](jungle-leopard-9.16.md)

Two real USBPcap captures from a support request on InfoPanel. The 9.16" unit uses the same
CDC serial ID as the JL Chill Arc 360, but its getDeviceInfo reply is a `{"cmd":"info","data":...}`
object with no `status` field, and the panel reports 1920x462, not 1920x480. Firmware 2.2 also
sends raw JPEGs with no size wrapper. That is why InfoPanel's handshake failed. The captures
are not stored here.
